The Mizumoto Lab participated in the 20th International Congress of the IUSSI in Freiburg, Germany, with two termite research talks and an organized symposium.
<!-- excerpt end -->

Nobu Mizumoto gave the invited talk **“Paired, individual, and collective-decision making by dispersing termites.”** Elijah Carroll presented **“Nesting biology drives the stability of tandem movement coordination in mating termites.”**

H. Li-Byarlay and N. Mizumoto also organized the symposium **“From founding to function: early-stage dynamics in the life cycle of social insects,”** a four-hour session featuring invited and contributed talks on the early stages of social insect colony life.
