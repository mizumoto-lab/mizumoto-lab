The Mizumoto Lab received an NSF award to study the mechanisms and evolution of collective nest construction in termites.
<!-- excerpt end -->

The project combines comparative behavioral experiments, international fieldwork, video analysis, modeling, and phylogenetic analysis. It will also support two PhD students beginning in Fall 2027.

[Learn more about NSF Award 2611846](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award?AWD_ID=2611846).
