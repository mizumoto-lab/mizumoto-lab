Our new paper documents four decades of Formosan subterranean termite (*Coptotermes formosanus*) expansion in Alabama.
<!-- excerpt end -->

The species is now documented in 32 of Alabama's 67 counties, with inland expansion strongly associated with interstate highways. These records provide a baseline for continued monitoring of this invasive termite in the state.

[Read the paper in *Urban Ecosystems*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11252-026-02107-z) (DOI: 10.1007/s11252-026-02107-z).

Explore the records and current distribution through the [Alabama Termite Map](https://mizumoto-lab.github.io/AL-termite-map/), and access the [data and analysis code on GitHub](https://github.com/nobuaki-mzmt/FST-AL-distribution).
